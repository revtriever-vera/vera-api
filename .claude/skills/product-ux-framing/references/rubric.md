# Rubric

Questions for evaluating a framing — someone else's, or your own before handing it off. The [foundations](foundations.md) explain the reasoning behind these questions. For a full audit, give each a verdict (holds, weak, missing, or not relevant and why), and for anything weak or missing, name the decision it puts at risk and what would close it. Order findings by how badly they could send the effort in the wrong direction.

1. **Situation and progress.** Is it clear whose progress this is and in what situation? Could the framing be wrong — or is it abstract enough to fit any product?
2. **Need behind the request.** If the work started as a request, has it been traced to the moment that produced it, with the request kept as one candidate?
3. **What they rely on today.** Are the current alternatives named, including doing nothing?
4. **Cost of change.** Does it account for what keeps people where they are, not only for what attracts them?
5. **Outcome.** Is success an observable change in what people do, with a stated link to the business and a stop condition decided in advance?
6. **Alternatives.** Was the chosen direction compared against others that differ in kind, and is the choice argued?
7. **Riskiest assumption.** Are the assumptions explicit and falsifiable, and is the one that could sink the idea addressed first, with a threshold set beforehand?
8. **Evidence.** Does each important claim show its source and strength? Is anything presented as evidence that is actually assumed or invented?
9. **Structure.** Are the product's things defined in the user's terms, with their relationships, consistently named?
10. **Worth and scope.** Is there a stated budget the solution fits, a path that delivers value end to end, and an explicit statement of what's out?
11. **Proportion.** Is the depth of framing matched to the cost and reversibility of being wrong?
12. **Lenses.** Did each method used change a decision? Which one?

For a focused change, use the questions that could change the product decision. Record actionable findings and their evidence in the existing review.
